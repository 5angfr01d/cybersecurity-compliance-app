Understanding Cybersecurity Compliance

Cybersecurity compliance involves adhering to regulations and standards that protect sensitive information. Here are some key standards:

  • GDPR: General Data Protection Regulation focuses on data privacy in the European Union.
  • HIPAA: Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act protects health information in the United States.
  • ISO 27001: An international standard for managing information security.
